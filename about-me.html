About Me

My name is Katebangira Warren. I was born on 22nd October 2003 in Kasese District, to Aman and Biira Night Juliet. I was raised in a God-fearing family under the Catholic Church, where I was taught discipline, respect, and responsibility. From a young age, I was encouraged to be a good example to other children, a value that still guides my life today.

My Academic Journey

My academic journey began at Kasese Primary School, where I completed my Primary Leaving Examination in 2012. In 2018, I joined Bugema Adventist Secondary School. It was there that I discovered my love for basketball and developed my talent in the sport. In 2020, I was honoured to be made the captain of the school basketball team. Unfortunately, my time as captain was cut short by the COVID-19 pandemic, which forced schools into a lockdown for almost two years.

When school resumed, I continued to grow as a leader. In 2023, I was elected by my fellow students as the Sanitary Prefect at Bugema Adventist Secondary School. That position taught me organization, teamwork, and how to serve others with humility. In 2024, I joined Kasese Moslem Secondary School for my Advanced Level, where I was again trusted with leadership as the Food Prefect.

In 2026, I joined Bugema University, where I am currently pursuing a Bachelor's Degree in Business Computing. At the university, I am an active member of the Fitness Club and the Basketball Club, which have helped me to stay disciplined, healthy, and a good team player. Academically, I am building strong skills in Programming using C, Web Designing, Database Management, and Accounting.

My dream is to combine my leadership experience, my love for sports, and my computing skills to become a responsible professional who creates solutions, inspires others, and makes my family and community proud.
